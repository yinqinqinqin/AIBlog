## Recommended preparation process.

- Make sure the module and content of the share/demo is stable and bug-free.

- Write WIKI documentation.

- Prepare sharing materials.

- The best form of sharing is live demo.

- Create a step-by-step tutorial sticker for IE to follow at https://code.byted.org/ies/amazing-js-dojo

- Contact @Hal to ask for a reasonable time to share and notify the learning group and US effect group a week in advance.
