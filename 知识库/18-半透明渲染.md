### Alpha Test

Alpha test is a totally-hard transparency technique, the pixels are either completely solid or completely clear, there are no soft edges.

![图片](https://yin-qin.oss-accelerate.aliyuncs.com/img/20260902152502123.png)

### Alpha To Coverage

Use alpha value as MSAA coverage. It's basically an anti-aliased alpha test, requiring MSAA enable to have a good result, but no need for depth sorting.

### Two Pass

Works best for situation when the center of a pack of objects is opaque but its outline is transparent, such as grass and hair

#### Method

- Pass1: Z-write on, color write off, alpha test on, for center area

- Pass2: Z-write off, color write on, alpha blend on, for outline area

### Weight Blended

Blending pixels without the correct order based on empirical relationship.

Theoretically, a correct blended pixel should be:

![图片](https://yin-qin.oss-accelerate.aliyuncs.com/img/20260902152502120.png)

In weight blended method, it skips the sorting of pixels in the correct order, and blend pixels all together:

![图片](https://yin-qin.oss-accelerate.aliyuncs.com/img/20260902152502119.png)

With some weight calculations that could work best:

![图片](https://yin-qin.oss-accelerate.aliyuncs.com/img/20260902152444791.png)

### Moment Based

Moment is the probability of the occlusion of current pixel by others. See the paper to get more details. (too complex for me :/)

Overall, it can achieve a little better results than weight blended, but both of them are based on empirical relationships and they get away from ground truth when the distances between objects are changing.

![图片](https://yin-qin.oss-accelerate.aliyuncs.com/img/20260902152444792.png)

### Depth Peeling

Requires two depth buffers to draw the scene multiple times. In each pass, it draws what's behind the previous layer and finally blend layers together.

![图片](https://yin-qin.oss-accelerate.aliyuncs.com/img/20260902152502122.gif)

> Depth peeling mini-app « Alec's Web Log
